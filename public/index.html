Bridging Students and Faculty Through Structured Academic Consultation

An institutional scheduling platform designed for Dalubhasaan ng Lungsod ng Lucena (DLL) to streamline consultation appointments, optimize faculty office hours, and foster academic collaboration.

System Overview

Standardized Academic Consultations

A transparent, reliable, and convenient platform built specifically for DLL’s academic community.

Faculty Office Hours

View verified consultation slots per faculty member, department, and specialization without needing to visit offices in person.

Structured 15–60 Minute Slots

Appointments are organized in dedicated intervals to prevent overlapping meetings, double bookings, and long waiting lines.

Automated Updates

Receive immediate alerts for appointment confirmations, faculty feedback remarks, schedule changes, or rescheduling.

Institutional Policy

Consultation Guidelines & Ethics

Important reminders for students and faculty regarding institutional consultation decorum.

01

Institutional Account Required

Only verified school accounts may access the system to file and approve consultations.

02

Specify Academic Purpose

Students must clearly state the consultation agenda (e.g. Capstone review, thesis guidance, course inquiries) upon requesting a slot.

03

Punctuality & Decorum

Both faculty and students are expected to adhere strictly to the booked consultation hour to respect other reserved slots.

04

Advance Cancellation

If unable to attend, cancel appointments at least 2 hours in advance so other students in need may book the vacated slot.
